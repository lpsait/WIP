BTEC Core Rules: (1) Feedback must NOT be issued to learners until the IV has verified the assessment decision. (2) Assignment briefs must be IV-approved before issue. Grading model: 60 GLH units — Pass = 6 pts, Merit = 10 pts, Distinction = 16 pts. All 8 units are 60 GLH. Maximum possible = 128 pts (all Distinctions). Certificate thresholds: Pass ≥54 | Merit ≥78 | Distinction ≥108. Criteria are hierarchical — D requires M+P; M requires P.

Assessment Criteria — Pass / Merit / Distinction
UnitPass CriteriaMerit CriteriaDistinction Criteria
Unit 1
Health and Safety in the Engineering Workplace		 State HASAWA provisions, identify safe practices, identify reporting procedures, carry out manual handling. Explain why practices are required, carry out risk assessment, explain roles under legislation. Evaluate H&S practice effectiveness, justify improvement recommendations.
Unit 2
Communications for Engineering Technicians		 Interpret engineering data/documentation, produce written communication, give verbal presentation. Explain use of engineering data in workplace, detailed written comms with technical terminology, coherent presentation. Evaluate communication methods for given context, produce comprehensive technical report to professional standard.
Unit 3
Mathematics for Engineering Technicians		 Solve problems using algebra, trigonometry, statistics and basic calculus. Solve more complex problems; apply trig to a practical problem; apply statistics to analyse engineering data. Solve complex problems using combined techniques; evaluate and validate mathematical solutions.
Unit 53
Operation and Maintenance of Light Vehicle Transmission Systems		 Describe manual/auto transmission principles, driveshaft and final drive construction, carry out inspection. Explain differences between transmission types for motorsport; diagnose fault from symptoms. Evaluate transmission configurations for motorsport; comprehensive inspection report.
Unit 54
Operation of Vehicle Chassis Systems		 Describe steering, suspension and braking system operating principles; carry out chassis inspection. Explain how chassis geometry affects motorsport handling; analyse inspection findings. Evaluate chassis setup impact on performance; produce chassis inspection and setup report.
Unit 44
Vehicle Engine Principles, Operation, Service and Repair		 Describe engine construction and operating principles; identify EMS components; carry out engine service. Explain EMS component interaction; carry out servicing and diagnose fault using scan tool. Evaluate EMS impact on performance and emissions; produce professional workshop report.
Unit 12
Engineering Drawing for Technicians		 Identify drawing standards; produce freehand sketches; produce formal engineering drawing. Detailed annotated sketches with dimensions/tolerances; formal drawing to BS8888 standard. Evaluate drawings for completeness and accuracy; produce professional drawing package.
Unit 13
Computer-aided Drafting in Engineering		 Identify CAD conventions and applications; produce 2D CAD drawing; produce 3D CAD model. Detailed 2D drawing with dimensions/tolerances/annotations; 3D assembly with exploded view. Evaluate CAD use in engineering context; produce professional CAD drawing package for manufacture.
