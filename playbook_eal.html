Motorsport
Engineering

Full delivery playbook for the EAL Level 2 Engineering qualification — motorsport pathway. 9 units, 540 GLH. All units: EWS1 (knowledge) + EWS2 (practical learner report). Unit 049 also requires EWS3. Pass or Referred only — no grade.

EWS1 + EWS2Evidence Model
⚠
Mandatory EAL Requirement: ALL teaching and assessment staff must hold documented evidence of occupational competence in the specific motorsport engineering areas they deliver and assess. This is not optional — it is an awarding body condition of registration. Evidence must be on file before delivery begins and available for EQA review.
📋
Assessment Model: Every unit requires EWS1 (short answer questions or online exam) + EWS2 (practical learner report). Unit 049 additionally requires EWS3. Both assessor and learner must sign and date every EWS declaration. Evidence must be cross-referenced to the specific learning outcome and assessment criteria. Evidence log updated throughout. Unit 001 EWS1 is an EAL online examination — book via EAL portal before September.

EOK2-001
MANDATORY
Engineering Environmental Awareness
60 GLH  |  Evidence: EWS1 (online exam) + EWS2 (practical)
LO1: H&S legislation (HASAWA, COSHH, PUWER, RIDDOR, manual handling)
LO2: Employer/employee roles, PDPs, discipline & grievance
LO3: Engineering org types, departmental functions, factors of industrial change
Assessment Target
18 Sep 2026
IQA Submit By
25 Sep 2026
⚠ Book EWS1 online exam via EAL portal BEFORE term starts. EWS2 practical (manual handling) assessed in session.
EOK2-048
OPTIONAL
Motorsport Engineering Techniques
60 GLH  |  Evidence: EWS1 (SAQ) + EWS2 (practical report)
LO1: Forms of communication in motorsport
LO2: IT/ICT in motorsport (data logging, telemetry, CAD)
LO3: Basic tools and techniques
LO4: Measurement and marking out
Assessment Target
2 Oct 2026
IQA Submit By
9 Oct 2026

EOK2-049
OPTIONAL
Motorsport Engineering Principles
60 GLH  |  Evidence: EWS1 (SAQ) + EWS2 (numeracy) + EWS3 (science)
LO1: Material selection for motorsport
LO2: Material properties, heat treatment, bonding
LO3: Numeracy (gear ratios, area, volume, formula transposition)
LO4: Science (SI units, forces, moments, friction, electricity, Ohm's law)
Assessment Target
16 Oct 2026
IQA Submit By
23 Oct 2026
⚠ THREE worksheets required. EWS2 = numeracy practical. EWS3 = science practical. All three signed.

OPTIONAL
Engine Ancillaries and Operating Systems

LO1: Engine principles and ancillary system layout
LO2: Function of ≥2 ancillary and ≥2 operating systems
LO3: Remove, inspect and refit ≥4 engine ancillary systems
LO4: Select and use correct tools throughout

6 Nov 2026

OPTIONAL
Motorsport Electrical Systems
60 GLH  |  Evidence: EWS1 (SAQ) + EWS2 (practical report)
LO1: DC power, auxiliary supply, alternator, starter system function

IQA Submit By

OPTIONAL
Chassis System Preparation
60 GLH  |  Evidence: EWS1 (SAQ) + EWS2 (practical report)
LO1: Motorsport vehicle design types (≥2)
LO2: Wheel and tyre characteristics and construction
LO3: Steering and suspension system function
LO4: Prepare chassis using data — corner weights, pressures, geometry
LO5: Select and use correct tools

Braking System Function and Inspection
60 GLH  |  Evidence: EWS1 (SAQ) + EWS2 (practical report)
LO1: Braking system operating principles, components, bleeding procedure, legal requirements
LO2: Inspect braking system; follow defect procedure; demonstrate brake bleed
LO3: Select data, tools and equipment; carry out braking inspection safely

OPTIONAL
Inspecting During an Event
60 GLH  |  Evidence: EWS1 (SAQ) + EWS2 (practical report)
LO1: H&S and housekeeping for vehicle inspection at an event
LO2: Working constraints during event inspection (instructions, deadlines, communication)
LO3: Pre-competition inspection, final inspection, post-inspection documentation
Assessment Target
12 Feb 2027
IQA Submit By
19 Feb 2027

EOK2-055
OPTIONAL
Transmission Principles and Operation
60 GLH  |  Evidence: EWS1 (SAQ) + EWS2 (practical report)
LO1: ≥1 transmission type, ≥1 clutch type, ≥1 final drive type — principles
LO2: Remove, inspect and refit gear ratio sets; confirm correct function
LO3: Remove, inspect and refit gear selection system; confirm correct function
Assessment Target
5 Mar 2027
IQA Submit By
12 Mar 2027
