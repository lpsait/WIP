01
Qualification Overview
What We're Delivering & Why It Matters
⚠️
Manager's Note — Read First
Delivery of these qualifications has taken significantly longer than it should. This playbook sets the standard for 2025–26. Every member of staff teaching on these programmes is expected to follow every step. There is no room for variation on the core process. If you have a question, escalate to the Coordinator — do not delay evidence collection.
Entry Level 3
City & Guilds Diploma in Vehicle System and Maintenance
Qualification ref: 3902. Covers 10 mandatory units (3902-001 through 3902-011). Each unit requires a practical observation AND a short-answer written assessment. Suitable for learners with little or no prior vehicle knowledge.
Level 1
City & Guilds Certificate in Vehicle Maintenance
Qualification ref: 7290-10. Mandatory units include health & safety (7290-001, 7290-031), knowledge units (7290-051, 7290-081), and vocational skills (e.g. 7290-702 Engine Cooling). Each unit has practical skills and knowledge evidence components.
Assessment Model
How Evidence Is Collected
Every unit has two evidence components: (1) an Assessor Observation Recording Form — completed during a practical task in the workshop, and (2) a Short Answer Questions Recording Form — completed in class or as a directed study activity. Both must be signed and dated.
Awarding Body
City & Guilds Registration
Learners must be registered on Walled Garden before assessment begins. Check registration status at the start of each unit. All portfolio evidence is submitted through the centre's IQA process before the City & Guilds claim deadline. Do not begin assessing unregistered learners.

Scheme of Work
Term-by-Term Unit Delivery Order
ℹ️
Why this order?
Units are sequenced to build mechanically on each other and to front-load health & safety compliance (a legal requirement). Staff must not skip ahead. Each unit's practical observation must be signed off before starting the next unit's worksheet.

Non-Negotiable Process
The Exact Steps Staff Must Follow — Every Unit, Every Time
🔴
This is not optional guidance — it is the required process
Every step below applies to every unit, for every learner. Shortcuts cause delays at IQA and risk failed claims with the awarding body. If you're unsure about any step, stop and contact the Coordinator before proceeding.
📋 Before the Unit Starts
  1. 1
    Confirm Learner Registration
    Log into City & Guilds Walled Garden. Verify every learner attending is registered for the correct qualification and unit. Do not assess unregistered learners — evidence will be void.
  2. 2
    Print & Prepare the Portfolio Pack
    Print the correct Assessor Observation Recording Form and Short Answer Questions Recording Form for the unit. Have them ready before the session — not during it.
  3. 3
    Risk Assessment Sign-Off
    For any practical unit involving the vehicle or equipment, ensure the relevant risk assessment is displayed in the workshop and signed by the learner before work begins. File the signed copy.
  4. 4
    Prepare Teaching Resources
    Select the teaching resources from Section 4 of this playbook that correspond to the unit. Share the VLE link or video in the session before the practical, not during it.
🔧 During the Practical Assessment
  1. 1
    Brief the Learner on the Task
    Explain exactly what the learner must demonstrate. Read out the observation criteria from the recording form so the learner knows what they are being assessed against before they begin.
  2. 2
    Observe — Do Not Assist
    During the assessed task, do not guide or correct the learner. Your role is to observe and record. If safety is at risk, stop the task and record it as a resubmission, not a pass.
  3. 3
    Complete the Observation Form in Real Time
    Tick each criteria as you observe it. Add written comments in every cell — do not leave cells blank. Record date, location, vehicle/equipment used, and witness details where required.
  4. 4
    Sign and Date Every Page
    Both assessor and learner must sign each page of the observation form on the day. Do not allow retrospective signing. IQA will reject unsigned or undated evidence.
✏️ Short Answer Questions (SAQ)
  1. 1
    Administer Under Controlled Conditions
    SAQs must be completed by the learner independently. No reference to notes, textbooks, or phones unless explicitly permitted by City & Guilds for that unit. Seat learners apart.
  2. 2
    Learner Completes Their Own Answers
    The learner must write in their own handwriting (or type, if a scribe arrangement is in place). Never transcribe answers on behalf of a learner without a formal access arrangement documented on their file.
  3. 3
    Mark and Record on the Form
    Mark each SAQ response against the marking guidance in the City & Guilds assessment specification. Record the mark on the form itself. Sign, date, and add your assessor number.
  4. 4
    Action Referrals Within 5 Working Days
    If a learner does not achieve the SAQ, issue a resubmission within 5 working days with targeted feedback. Do not leave learners with no-pass evidence sitting in folders unchallenged.
📁 After Each Unit — Portfolio & IQA
  1. 1
    File in the Portfolio Immediately
    Completed and signed observation form + completed and marked SAQ go into the learner's portfolio on the day of completion. Evidence that is not filed promptly gets lost.
  2. 2
    Update the Unit Tracker Worksheet
    Mark the unit as complete on the Candidate Unit Tracking Worksheet at the front of the portfolio. Initial and date the entry. This is what the IQA checks first.
  3. 3
    Submit to IQA Within One Week of Completion
    Do not batch units to submit at the end of the year. Submit each completed unit to the IQA within one week. Batching at year-end is a primary cause of delayed claims and is not acceptable practice.
  4. 4
    Act on IQA Feedback Immediately
    If the IQA flags missing evidence, gaps, or quality concerns, these must be resolved within 5 working days. Do not set IQA feedback aside and plan to address it later.

Assessment Guidance
What Good Evidence Looks Like — and What Will Be Rejected
✅ What IQA Expects to See
  • ✓
    Assessor observation form fully completed — no blank cells
  • ✓
    Assessor and learner signatures on every page, dated on the day of assessment
  • ✓
    Written assessor comments that specifically describe what the learner did to meet each criterion
  • ✓
    SAQ responses in the learner's own words, marked and annotated by the assessor
  • ✓
    Risk assessment signed by the learner before the practical activity begins
  • ✓
    Unit tracker worksheet updated with assessor initials and date
  • ✓
    Learner registration confirmed on Walled Garden before evidence is collected
  • ✓
    For L1 7290-001: risk rating documented using the 0–6 matrix (0–2 low, 3–4 medium, 5–6 high)
❌ Common Reasons Evidence is Rejected
  • ✗
    Observation form signed retrospectively — not on the day of the observed task
  • ✗
    Assessor comments that simply repeat the criterion ("learner completed the task") — no specific detail
  • ✗
    SAQ answers that appear identical across multiple learners — suspected collusion
  • ✗
    Missing learner signature on any page
  • ✗
    Risk assessment not present or signed after the practical has taken place
  • ✗
    Unregistered learner assessed — evidence cannot be claimed
  • ✗
    Portfolios submitted to IQA with multiple units incomplete — batching at year end
  • ✗
    Evidence from a demonstration by the assessor counted as learner evidence
📝 Writing Good Assessor Observation Comments

The most common quality failure is vague assessor comments. Below are examples of unacceptable versus acceptable evidence statements.

❌ Unacceptable
"Learner completed the task correctly."

"Task was carried out to a satisfactory standard."

"The learner demonstrated the required skills."

"Good."

"Pass."
✅ Acceptable
"Learner correctly identified the engine oil dipstick, removed it, wiped it clean, reinserted fully and checked the oil level against the min/max markings."

"Learner used a coolant pressure tester to pressurise the cooling system to 1 bar and held pressure for 2 minutes with no leaks identified."

"Learner correctly selected PPE (gloves, safety glasses) before beginning brake pad removal and verbally explained the reason for each item when prompted."

Key Dates & Deadlines
Milestones That Cannot Slip
📅
Missing the claim deadline means learners do not achieve. There are no extensions.
City & Guilds claim deadlines are fixed. If portfolios are not IQA-signed and submitted before the deadline, the learner does not receive their certificate that year. This has happened before. It will not happen again.
🗓 Recommended Internal Timeline
September — Week 1
Learner Registration on Walled Garden
All learners registered before the first assessment. Coordinator to confirm. No exceptions.
September — Week 2
Portfolios Issued & Introduction Session
Each learner receives their portfolio. Staff walk through the unit structure and explain how evidence is collected. Learners sign the declaration page.
October — End of Month
Units 1–3 Complete & Submitted to IQA
First three units in the scheme of work must be assessed, filed, and IQA-ready by this date.
December — End of Term
Units 4–6 Complete & IQA-Sampled
Mid-point check. IQA to produce interim sampling report. Any gaps or quality issues to be resolved in January — not left to summer.
March — End of Month
All Units Complete — Internal Deadline
Every unit must be assessed and in the portfolio by this date. This gives 8 weeks of IQA time before the claim window.
May — Early
IQA Sign-Off Complete
IQA to have sampled all portfolios and issued sign-off. Any remaining actions resolved this week only.
May — Mid (Check Walled Garden)
Results Claim Submitted to City & Guilds
Coordinator submits results claim. Learners achieve. Certificates processed by City & Guilds within 4–6 weeks of claim.
🚨 Escalation Protocol

If any of the following occur, the staff member must notify the Coordinator that same day — not at the next team meeting, not by email later in the week. Same day.

Escalate Immediately If:
A learner fails to attend the practical assessment session
Escalate Immediately If:
Suspected collusion is identified in SAQ responses
Escalate Immediately If:
A learner is found to be unregistered on Walled Garden
Escalate Immediately If:
Evidence has been lost, damaged, or cannot be located
Escalate Immediately If:
IQA flags a serious quality concern on a portfolio
