Year 2

Year 2 of the two-year BTEC Level 3 Motorsport pathway. Continuing cohort from Year 1. 8 units including Unit 4 Engineering Project (120 GLH) which runs the full academic year. All submissions before the May half term. Graded Pass, Merit or Distinction.

⚠
BTEC Core Rules: (1) Feedback must NOT be issued until IV verified. (2) Assignment briefs IV-approved before issue. (3) Authenticity declaration signed for every submission. (4) Resubmission permitted once. (5) No submissions after 31 May 2027. Unit 4 Engineering Project: topic must be negotiated and approved by the assessor before the learner begins any work.
📐
Grading: 60 GLH units — P=6pts, M=10pts, D=16pts. Unit 4 (120 GLH) — P=12pts, M=20pts, D=32pts. Extended Diploma thresholds: Pass ≥108 | Merit ≥156 | Distinction ≥216. Maximum possible = 128pts (all Distinctions). Criteria hierarchical — D requires M+P; M requires P.
🏎
Unit 4 Engineering Project — starts Day 1: The 120 GLH project runs across the full year. Topic negotiation begins Week 1 (7 Sep). Project plan approved by Week 4. Report due 16 May 2027. Verbal presentations 17–21 May 2027. IV complete by 30 May. All before half term. Align with ESR CityCar Cup for authentic evidence where possible.

Assessment Criteria — Pass / Merit / Distinction
UnitPass CriteriaMerit CriteriaDistinction Criteria
Unit 4
Engineering Project
120 GLH		 Produce project plan; carry out activities; record outcomes; present in written report. Detailed plan with justified methodology; analyse data against objectives; evaluate process and outcomes. Critically evaluate methodology and justify alternatives; produce comprehensive report and presentation to professional standard.
Unit 45
Vehicle System Fault Diagnosis and Rectification
60 GLH		 Describe diagnostic principles; identify diagnostic system components; carry out diagnostic routine recording findings. Explain live data analysis use in diagnosis; carry out targeted tests explaining diagnostic logic. Evaluate diagnostic process effectiveness with justification; produce comprehensive diagnostic report to professional standard.
Unit 46
Applications of Vehicle Science and Mathematics
60 GLH		 Solve vehicle science problems using maths; apply dynamics to performance parameters; apply thermodynamics/fluid mechanics. Explain mathematical relationships governing motorsport performance; analyse performance data. Evaluate vehicle performance using combined methods; produce technical analysis of motorsport data.
Unit 49
Function and Operation of Vehicle Petrol Injection Systems
60 GLH		 Describe petrol injection construction and principles; identify fuel metering and ignition control; carry out inspection. Explain ECU use of sensor inputs; carry out diagnostic tests and interpret results. Evaluate injection calibration impact on performance and emissions; comprehensive diagnostic report.
Unit 51
Operation and Testing of Vehicle Electronic Ignition Systems
60 GLH		 Describe electronic ignition construction and principles; explain timing control; carry out tests recording results. Analyse test results and explain diagnostic significance; carry out oscilloscope analysis and interpret waveforms. Evaluate ignition performance using test data and oscilloscope; comprehensive diagnostic report.
Unit 52
Vehicle Engine Management Systems
60 GLH		 Describe EMS construction and operation; explain control strategies; carry out EMS tests recording results. Analyse EMS live data to diagnose fault; carry out structured diagnostic routine. Evaluate EMS strategy impact on performance and emissions; comprehensive EMS diagnostic report.
Unit 105
Motorsport Workshop Practices
60 GLH		 Identify H&S/housekeeping requirements; identify tools/equipment/documentation; carry out maintenance activity. Explain why practices are critical in motorsport context; demonstrate professional-standard practice and documentation. Evaluate practices against professional team standards; produce professional-standard workshop documentation.
Unit 106
Motorsport Vehicle Preparation and Inspection
60 GLH		 Identify pre-event preparation requirements; identify inspection requirements; carry out preparation with documentation. Explain why each requirement is critical for safety and performance; complete full preparation and post-event inspection. Evaluate process against professional standards; produce comprehensive preparation and inspection report.
